WHY J.I.M?

More Money. More Meaning.
More Freedom.

J.I.M was built around a simple belief: business ownership should be financially rewarding, personally fulfilling, and capable of creating more freedom.

More Money

We've designed a lean, technology-enabled studio model with low overheads, recurring revenue, and strong unit economics—helping owners build profitable, scalable businesses.

More Meaning

Every day, J.I.M owners help people move more, improve their health, build confidence, and connect with their local community. Success isn't measured solely by revenue, but by the impact created along the way.

More Freedom

Powered by technology and automation, J.I.M is designed to reduce operational complexity and create flexibility for owners. Whether you choose to run a single studio or grow into multiple locations, the model is built to support a lifestyle as well as a business.

Founded by Jarad Hobbs, J.I.M is the result of more than two decades in the fitness industry and a simple question:

"How do we create a fitness business that's better for members, better for owners, and capable of reaching more people?"

The answer became J.I.M a technology-enabled fitness concept designed to inspire more people to move while creating an opportunity for owners to build a profitable, purpose-driven business.

Ownership That Fits
Your Lifestyle

“Some owners choose to run their studio day-to-day. Others lead through a manager or grow into multiple locations. The right model depends on your goals, experience, and stage of life”.

Owner-Manager

Own and run your studio

For ambitious operators who want to build a business and be involved in the day-to-day success of the studio.

  • Own the business and manage the studio
  • Manage the member experience and local community
  • Build a career through business ownership

Owner-Leader

Lead the business while a manager runs the studio

For business owners who want to focus on growth, leadership, and performance rather than daily operations.

  • Employ and lead a full-time Studio Manager
  • Focus on growth, marketing, and business performance
  • Stay actively involved as the business owner

Multi-Site Owner

Build and lead multiple studios

For experienced operators looking to scale and create a portfolio of locations.

  • Lead multiple studios through a team of managers
  • Focus on people, systems, and performance
  • Build a scalable business with long-term growth potential

FAQ’S

What is the upfront investment?

A typical J.I.M studio investment ranges from $200,000 to $250,000, which includes turnkey fit-out, certified infrared equipment, pre-sale launch marketing, and working capital.

What are the ongoing fees?

Our competitive franchise royalty and marketing fund contributions are designed to ensure head office continuously drives customer acquisition and software improvements.

What support do I receive?

You receive complete end-to-end guidance from real estate selection and lease negotiation to studio construction, staff hiring, instructor training, and automated systems.

What kind of return can I expect?

Historical mature studios operate with strong 30-35% cash operating margins with rapid member acquisition through our pre-sale methodology.

I’ve never done sculpt, stretch and strengthen. Does that matter?

Not at all! We provide exhaustive instructor training and class format playbooks so your studio delivers exceptional, consistent experiences from day one.

Do I need previous fitness industry experience?

No. Many of our highest-performing franchise owners come from corporate, sales, hospitality, or management backgrounds with a passion for people and wellness.
