FAQ’S

First Time

Do I need my own mat?

No, we provide premium sanitized yoga mats, blocks, resistance bands, and foam rollers for every single class.

What should I bring to class?

Just bring a water bottle, a sweat towel, and comfortable workout clothes. We have water refill stations in every studio.

What do I wear to class?

Breathable workout attire such as activewear shorts, leggings, or tops that you feel comfortable sweating in.

Do I need to wear shoes?

Most of our classes are performed barefoot or with grip socks to improve balance, foot strength, and joint alignment.

I’ve never done sculpt, stretch and strengthen. Does that matter?

Not at all! Our classes are low-impact and designed for all fitness levels with clear coach modifications for every move.

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive 10-15 minutes before your class start time so our team can show you around the studio and set up your mat.

Membership

How do I sign up for a free class?

Download the J.I.M App, select your home studio, and your complimentary first class pass will be ready to book immediately.

How do I sign up for my membership?

Memberships can be set up directly in the J.I.M app or with our studio team before or after your first session.

Do you offer class packs?

Yes, we offer flexible 10-class and 20-class packs in addition to unlimited weekly membership tiers.

Do I need a booking to attend a 30 minute workout session?

Yes, because studio mat spots are capped to ensure plenty of personal space, bookings via the app are required.

Can I bring a friend?

Absolutely! All unlimited members receive guest pass perks every month to introduce friends and family to J.I.M.

Infrared Workouts

What is infrared heat?

Infrared heat gently penetrates muscle tissue directly rather than merely blowing hot air, providing deeper muscle warmth, circulation, and recovery.

Are infrared workouts safe?

Yes! Infrared wavelengths are the same therapeutic rays used in physical therapy and neonatal units, free of UV radiation.

What are the benefits of infrared?

Benefits include enhanced calorie burn, accelerated muscle recovery, improved cardiovascular health, deeper sleep, and radiant skin.

How often should I attend J.I.M?

Because our workouts are low-impact, 3 to 5 times per week is ideal to maximize physical results without overtraining joints.

Can I do J.I.M if I am pregnant?

We recommend consulting with your obstetrician prior to heated workouts during pregnancy. We also offer non-heated movement modifications.

Health & Safety

What’s your cleaning protocol?

Studios are hospital-grade sanitized between every class, including deep infrared mat cleans, touchpoint disinfections, and HEPA air filtering.

What disinfectants / chemicals do you use?

We use eco-certified, non-toxic, antibacterial botanical solutions that are tough on bacteria yet gentle on skin and breathing.

What about air quality?

Our studios feature high-volume fresh air ventilation systems that cycle and replace 100% of studio air continuously throughout each hour.

What if I test positive for Covid-19?

Please stay home and rest! We will gladly place your membership on an immediate complimentary hold until you make a full recovery.
