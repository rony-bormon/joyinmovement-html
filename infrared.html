THE BENEFIT
OF INFRARED

J.I.M uses infrared heating to create a warm 40°C workout environment, bringing heat and movement together in every 30-minute class. It warms the body, supports circulation, helps muscles loosen up and can support recovery - all while helping you work up a serious sweat.

Research also shows that infrared heat used around exercise can increase peripheral blood flow, which may help reduce muscle soreness and support neuromuscular recovery, while also supporting calorie burn, sleep, skin and energy.
